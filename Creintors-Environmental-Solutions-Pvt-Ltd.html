Vision & Mission

Creintors Environmental Solutions' purpose is rooted in dependable treatment standards and trust-building environmental outcomes for every facility we serve.

CEPL Vision

Vision

To be a beacon of innovation for the holistic architecture and integration of drinking water, effluent, sewage and solid waste management systems that deliver sustainable solutions with a carbon-negative impact.

CEPL Mission

Mission

Through the culture of innovation and collaboration of people and technologies, building an ecosystem of offerings, that deliver sustainable solutions addressing the pains for assuring quality water generation and distribution with accountability, for treating effluent, sewage and solid waste by being closest to the point of generation of waste thus creating the sense of awareness and participation

PROCESS CATEGORY 01

Core Treatment Processes

Primary treatment blocks that handle separation, settling, mixing and core process conditioning for stable downstream performance.

Solid Liquid Separation Unit

Dewatering-focused systems for sludge handling and disposal reduction, including Rotary Drum Filter, Centrifuge and Hydrocyclone options.

Sedimentation

Settling-based suspended particle removal systems including Clariflocculators, High Rate Clarifiers and DAF-integrated settling modules.

Mixing

Application-matched mixing systems such as pressured water jets, inline static mixers, hydraulic mixers and mechanical mixers.

Chemical Dosing System

Skid-mounted dosing systems with corrosion-resistant construction and dosing pump variants including plunger, diaphragm and metering pumps.

Screening & Aeration

Front-end protection and oxidation modules including coarse/fine/grit screening and cascade, spray, diffuser and packed-column aeration systems.

Dissolved Air Floatation (DAF)

Clarification process for removal of oils, grease and suspended contaminants. Includes compact circular DAF and broad industrial application coverage.

PROCESS CATEGORY 02

Automation & Distribution Systems

Control, monitoring and modular deployment systems that improve reliability, accountability and operating continuity across water networks.

Fabricated Modular Plants

Fast-deployment modular treatment plants with expandable capacity, lower civil dependency and operation-focused scalability for small to medium MLD needs.

Water Distribution Scheme

Automation-led storage, pumping and regulated distribution with flow control and pressure balancing for reliable destination-level delivery.

Flow Monitoring & Metering

Mechanical, ultrasonic and electromagnetic flow systems with AMR/AMI readiness, monitoring, reporting and online accountability features.

Process Automation & Installations

Site-level process automation and integrated field deployment support to sustain uptime, operational visibility and plant reliability.

Field Services & Support

Operations, commissioning and maintenance support built for practical performance continuity across treatment and distribution systems.

PROCESS CATEGORY 03

Advanced Polishing & Sustainability

Final-stage purification and discharge-minimization solutions designed for compliance, reuse quality and long-term environmental performance.

Online Hydraulic Filter

Automated filtration with self-cleaning and differential-pressure-led backwash cycles, including Rapid Sand Filter Modules and Membrane Filtration (RO).

Filtration

Final suspended-solids polishing stage using graded media and pressure sand filter systems engineered for high impurity removal efficiency.

Disinfection

End-stage disinfection options including Electrochlorinator, Ozonation, UV and Gas Chlorinator systems for safe treated water output.

Zero Liquid Discharge

Integrated ZLD schemes where wastewater is treated and recycled through complete process loops, minimizing and targeting zero end discharge.
