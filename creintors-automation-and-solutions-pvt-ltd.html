About CASPL

Automation Systems Built for Modern Manufacturing

Creintors Automation and Solutions Pvt. Ltd. builds practical, future-ready automation ecosystems for industrial operations. We combine controls engineering, connected plant intelligence, and custom machine design to deliver solutions that improve traceability, reduce downtime, and unlock measurable process efficiency.

From standalone automation cells to plant-wide integration, our team focuses on systems that are robust in production, intuitive for operators, and scalable for the next stage of growth.

Featured Product

FOCUS-270 – Smart Mobile Storage System

FOCUS-270 is built for secure, high-speed mobile storage in industrial and institutional environments where access control, accountability, and operational efficiency matter every day.

  • Secure storage for up to 270 mobile devices in one controlled system.
  • Biometric fingerprint access paired with a responsive touchscreen interface.
  • Fast 20 to 30 second retrieval and deposit cycle for daily users.
  • Eliminates manual tracking and improves device-level visibility.
  • Integrates with attendance systems for stronger operational linkage.
  • Ideal for factories, institutions, controlled facilities, and large teams.
High Accountability Storage
